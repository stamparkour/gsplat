You may edit/add/remove any file inside the lib/render and lib/train subsystem.

lib/render is dedicated to displaying content to the user
lib/train is for training the 3DGS.

It is up to you to where you want to place the 'rendering scene' API.

you will recieve at initialization:
- VkInstance
- VkPhysicalDevice
- VkDevice
- VkQueue
- VkDescriptorPool

you should get above by 10/31/26. If you need it earlier contact Eli

At runtime you will use your Projection Matrix and Camera Transformation Matrix to the 'rendering scene' API and recieve a VkImage. you will render this VkImage.
